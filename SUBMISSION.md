# Touchline — final release submission text

Touchline is an evidence-grounded football companion built with Microsoft Foundry. A viewer selects the spell they missed, receives a short explanation and can inspect why it was selected. Quick and Tactical styles, team/player focus and an optional connected match report serve different reading needs without changing the facts.

The single investigator chooses read-only evidence tools under call, time and token budgets. Tools compare roles, substitutions, progression, possession, formations and disciplinary changes with denominators and competing explanations. Checked references and local narrative composition reduce unsupported output; unavailable model access produces a labelled local explanation. Reports make one optional context-selection call, with source-owned chronology and wording.

All football input is fictional. Period-aware replay includes announced minimum added time and the actual final whistle. Uploaded version 2 packages share the same evidence path as built-in matches; version 1 remains supported. Optional decision explanations attribute short IFAB law excerpts and distinguish recorded decisions from independent adjudication. Formation dots are schematic; illustrative chance quality is not calibrated xG.

Our distinctive combination is understandable commentary, inspectable football evidence, corrections and missing-evidence handling, temporal boundaries, and deliberate refusal to turn a statistical association into a causal claim. Reproducible comparison results include failures and fallbacks; we do not claim superiority, real-match validation or an independent fan study.
