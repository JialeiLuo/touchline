# Demonstration video script — target 105 seconds

0–10: Touchline is an AI co-commentator for the fan who missed part of a match. It explains the story behind a selected interval, with evidence you can inspect.

10–25: This is an entirely fictional 90-minute match. Replay events update the score, statistics and current lineups. No broadcast footage or live football feed is connected.

25–45: The substitution example jumps to minute 66. Ellis has come on as a right winger. Choose minutes 55–66, Tactical detail and Ellis, then ask what changed.

45–65: Touchline connects the declared role with supplied history: only 20 of 305 prior minutes in that role. It also examines complete attacking sequences and matched involvement windows. Source links show exactly what supports each story.

65–80: Quick mode is shorter. Team and player focus change relevance while preserving major incidents and facts. A translated glossary helps fans discuss football across languages.

80–95: Upload a documented synthetic JSON package. Availability times and corrections keep reported incidents distinct from confirmed rules. Changing the selection flags an old explanation.

95–105: Microsoft Foundry ranks supported stories; Azure hosts the public demo. Calculations and wording stay inspectable. This is bounded AI selection, not autonomous tactical investigation. Try Touchline through the public demo and repository.
